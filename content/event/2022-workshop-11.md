Led a two-hour, live workshop covering the basics of building reproducible workflows
with the `targets` workflow management package for R using National Ecological Observatory Network data as an example. This workshop was part of
Day 2: Data Science in the
[DSOS/AEMON-J Hacking Limnology 2022 Virtual Summit & Workshops](https://aquaticdatasciopensci.github.io/) event.
Materials are archived on [OSF](https://osf.io/682v5/).
