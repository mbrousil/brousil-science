My employer, the Radical Open Science Syndicate (ROSS) at Colorado State University,  collaborated with the EPA on its development of the Tools for Automated Data Analysis (EPATADA) R package. EPATADA helps States, Tribal Nations and other stakeholders more efficiently compile and evaluate Water Quality Portal (WQP) data.

ROSS developed powerful new geospatial functions and optimized data retrieval methods for the package—enhancing EPATADA’s already powerful water quality analysis tools. Through this webinar I demonstrated the enhanced EPATADA package in action, including: 

+ Querying the WQP using `sf` objects or tribal info
+ Converting WQP data into `sf` objects
+ Associating WQP monitoring locations with ATTAINS assessment units
